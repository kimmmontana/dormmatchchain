Who we are

DormMatch is a dormitory booking platform that connects students with safe, affordable, and well-located accommodation, with every booking managed in one place.

Our shared-room waitlist gives residents visibility of who they will be living with before they commit. Our solo rooms offer private accommodation that is confirmed as soon as payment is verified.

How booking works

From sign up to move in, in four steps.

1

Create an account

Sign up with your school details so dorms and roommates know who you are.

2

Choose a dorm

Compare location, price, and capacity across shared and solo rooms.

3

Pay and submit

Scan the QR code and enter your payment reference, or pay in XLM with a Stellar wallet where available.

4

Track your booking

Follow your booking's status anytime under My Bookings.

What your booking status means

Verify

We received your booking and are checking your payment.

Waitlisted

Your payment is verified and you're in line for a shared room.

Approved

Your booking is confirmed. Shared rooms are approved once the waitlist reaches capacity.

Declined

The booking wasn't confirmed by the dorm administrator.
